07 · Takeaways

Four rules for faster proof.

Rule 01

Recommend, don’t remove

What I saw
Leading with a better path beat deleting the old one.
Next time
Keep a fallback whenever you promote a new default.
Rule 02

Design once, configure by market

What I saw
One pattern covered the US and international markets.
Next time
Build the shared pattern before the first market ships.
Rule 03

Show the next step

What I saw
A segmented bar made a long task feel finite.
Next time
Name the next action on every screen.
Rule 04

Make the fast path the default

What I saw
Connect to Bank led, upload stayed one tap away.
Next time
Default to speed, never to a dead end.
