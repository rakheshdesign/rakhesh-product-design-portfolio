08 · Takeaways

Four rules from six markets.

Rule 01

Context beats the metric

What I saw
The funnel blamed the landing page. The test showed it was teaching.
Next time
Pair every funnel read with why people leave.
Rule 02

Show the math

What I saw
UAT: no mental math, no confusion, no dilemma.
Next time
Say what the entry will do, as people type.
Rule 03

Compare like with like

What I saw
Completion differs because each flow asks for different things.
Next time
Agree a per-step measure with Product before launch.
Rule 04

Instrument the mental model

What I saw
The mismatch surfaced through calls and surveys, not dashboards.
Next time
Track “typed below current limit” from day one.
