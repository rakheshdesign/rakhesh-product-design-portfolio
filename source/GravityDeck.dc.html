05 · Takeaways

Four rules from one building block.

Rule 01

One material beats many parts

What I saw
Making everything from balls let any slide morph into any other.
Next time
Pick a single building block before adding features.
Rule 02

Remove the first hurdle

What I saw
No install and no sign-up got people to the stage in one visit.
Next time
Ask what stands between the idea and the first result.
Rule 03

Offer more than one way in

What I saw
Describe, import or pick a layout. People start in different places.
Next time
Design the on-ramps, not just the road.
Rule 04

Keep the decisions

What I saw
AI built fast. The concept and the calls stayed mine.
Next time
Let AI build fast. Own the concept and the final calls.
