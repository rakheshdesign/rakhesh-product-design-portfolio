    Funnel data, user tests, competitor teardown, what market teams know.

  2. 2

    Learn

    Competing hypotheses, journey maps, synthesis with Product, Risk and Business.

  3. 3

    Make

    Several directions, AI-assisted MVPs, critiques, then tests in production.

About

A little about how I think and work.

I’m Rakhesh, a Product Designer in Bangalore, at American Express. I design financial services, enterprise products, design systems and AI experiences, from strategy to hands-on craft.

I care about curiosity, careful problem-solving, and the belief that good design makes complex things easier. I’ve designed across fintech, HealthTech, consumer e-commerce and advertising, for business and consumer products.
