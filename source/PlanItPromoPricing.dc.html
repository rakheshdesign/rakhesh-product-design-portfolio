07 · Takeaways

Four rules for scaling an offer.

Rule 01

Variables beat copy and paste

What I saw
Market strings in variables meant a new market is a small change.
Next time
Set up variables before the second market, not the fifth.
Rule 02

Default to what helps

What I saw
The promo duration is selected, so eligible offers aren’t missed.
Next time
Pre-select the best-for-the-customer option, and say why.
Rule 03

Show both prices

What I saw
The undiscounted amount keeps the offer honest and easy to compare.
Next time
Never show a saving without its baseline.
Rule 04

Step aside when it doesn’t apply

What I saw
Non-promo choices hide promo details.
Next time
Remove the message when the condition goes away.
