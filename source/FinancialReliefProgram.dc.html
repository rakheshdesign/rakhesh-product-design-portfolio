07 · Takeaways

Four rules from the funnel.

Rule 01

Spend where it bleeds

What I saw
Nine steps, and the biggest drops sat in just a few of them.
Next time
Map the drops before scoping the work.
Rule 02

Removing beats adding

What I saw
Cutting the review step drove the largest drop-off reduction.
Next time
Ask what each screen earns before polishing it.
Rule 03

Let people compare to choose

What I saw
Both plans in one view won the crit, 6 to 1. No toggling, no remembering figures.
Next time
Show money, term and APR together.
Rule 04

Bring the gates in early

What I saw
Risk, Compliance and Tech notes shaped six changes before build.
Next time
Invite them before the first layout, not after.
