07 · Takeaways

Four rules for clear notices.

Rule 01

Tie the notice to the choice

What I saw
A disclaimer that changes with the selection gets read.
Next time
Place each disclosure beside the decision it affects.
Rule 02

Uplift, don’t patch

What I saw
A patched flow keeps its old problems.
Next time
Compare patch and uplift on cost of staying put.
Rule 03

Keep navigation still

What I saw
Stable navigation makes a flow feel short.
Next time
Fix the frame before the content.
Rule 04

Local needs, global pattern

What I saw
Mexico adapted the shared pattern instead of forking it.
Next time
Adapt first. Fork only when forced.
