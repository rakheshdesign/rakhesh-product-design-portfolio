Slides thatassemble themselves.

Gravity is a web presentation tool where every title, icon, chart and table is built from the same fixed-size balls. Because everything is made of the same thing, one slide can morph into the next.

The editor

A slide rail, a canvas, and motion that suggests itself

Slides on the left, the stage in the middle, and transitions with a recommended pick on the right.

  • A toolbar for title, text, icon, shape, bar, line, pie, table and image
  • Gravity recommends a transition for each slide and shows it on hover
  • A ball budget on the slide, so the stage never gets too dense

The editor

Describe the deck, then choose the tone

A short dialog turns a topic and optional notes into a deck.

  • Number of slides: 5, 8, 10 or 12
  • Tone: Confident, Friendly, Playful, Formal or Inspiring
  • Replace the deck or add to it. The AI only uses real figures you give it
