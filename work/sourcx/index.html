03 · The designs

Real screens. Real flows.

These are my SourcX designs from Figma. Open any of them as a live prototype.

Client and provider entry

One front door, two paths

The website explains the platform and sends each audience to its own next step.

  • A clear promise: get the right service providers effortlessly
  • Two calls to action in the header: find a partner, or sign up as a provider
  • A product preview right in the hero, so the AI search is visible at once
Open in Figma
Client experience

Ask in plain language

A chat-style search turns a need into a list of rated companies and individuals.

  • Example searches remove the blank-page problem
  • A history of requests, plus Proposals and Shortlists, one tap away
  • One prompt box, with attachments for richer briefs
Open in Figma
Client experience

Results you can act on

Each match is a card with the facts that matter, and the next step is on the card.

  • Rating, specialization, size, budget range and location at a glance
  • Request for Proposal and View Company on every card
  • Compare all, or send a Request for Proposal to all companies
Open in Figma
Admin experience

A dashboard that rewards a complete profile

Once listed, a provider sees how the platform is working for them.

  • A clear confirmation that the company is listed
  • Searches, proposals and notifications in three quick tiles
  • A profile-completeness ring with one action to add details
Open in Figma
Admin experience

List a company in three ways in

Providers can create an account, log in, or claim a profile that already exists.

  • Claim Company Profile covers providers who are already on the platform
  • A side panel states the value: be discovered, connect with clients, build trust
  • One focused choice per screen keeps setup short
