04 · Canada · English + French · a mental-model gap

A small gap. A big mental-model problem. Now validated.

Canada launched all four capabilities in English and French. Then a pattern showed up: people meaning to increase their limit were submitting a decrease. Watch what the system reads, or tap to hold a case.

The decision

It affected 0.2% of customers, so I did not redesign the flow for everyone. I made the system do the math.

Making everyone enter a top-up would have changed the experience for the other 99.8%. Instead I made the system’s interpretation explicit and put the care at the one moment it matters: a calculated confirmation that shows the current limit, the change and the new limit.
